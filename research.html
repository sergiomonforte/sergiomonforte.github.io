Research

My work sits within formal syntax and its interfaces, focusing mainly on Basque. I study how discourse and modal particles, question formation, and sentence adverbs are organised in the clause, and what this can tell us about the relation between syntax, semantics, and discourse — especially at the periphery of the clause.

A second strand of my research looks at dialectal microvariation, using it as a window onto the fine-grained syntactic structure of Basque across varieties and border areas (e.g. the Navarrese–Lapurdian dialect continuum).

My medium- and long-term goals are to deepen the connections between syntax and discourse — particularly the syntax of the Basque left periphery and the expression of modality — and to further explore how question syntax interacts with pragmatics, as well as how it changes over time.
