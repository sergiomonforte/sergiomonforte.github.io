Sergio Monforte

I am currently Associate Professor in the Department of Basque Language and Communication at the University of the Basque Country (EHU). My research focuses on Basque syntax — questions, discourse particles, and related phenomena. I am also interested in generational variation in these features: whether they have changed over time, and in which direction. In addition, I am interested in the development of academic Basque.

I teach academic and scientific–technical communication in Basque, and I also collaborate as an associate lecturer at the Université de Pau et des Pays de l’Adour (Baiona campus), teaching Basque Linguistics.

Research interests

  • 01

    Basque syntax

    Structure, agreement, and the architecture of the Basque clause.

  • 02

    Discourse & modal particles

    How particles like -a, al, and ote encode modality and discourse meaning.

  • 03

    Question formation

    The syntax of polar and non-canonical questions.

  • 04

    Syntax–pragmatics interface

    Where sentence structure meets discourse and interpretation.

  • 05

    Dialectal microvariation

    Fine-grained syntactic variation across Basque-speaking areas.

  • 06

    Left-periphery phenomena

    Formal approaches to modeling the syntax of the clause edge.
